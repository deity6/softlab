SOFT LAB — third-party sources
==============================

SOFT LAB is a personal project made for study and exchange, and NOT for
commercial use. Several effects come from other open-source projects; each is
listed below with its original link so you can trace it upstream yourself.

Full list: https://github.com/deity6/softlab/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY.md
中文版：THIRD_PARTY.zh-CN.md


--- three.js — MIT --------------------------------------------------------
Copyright (c) 2010-2025 three.js authors
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE
Used for the 3D soft-body renderer and the RGBE HDR texture loader.


--- Luffyzhang2016 / soft-matter ------------------------------------------
https://github.com/Luffyzhang2016/soft-matter
Rigid-body motion, deformable shell, shape generators and the HDRI refraction
shader behind the Soft Matter experiment.
The upstream repository carries no LICENSE file.


--- DavidHDev / canvas-ui -------------------------------------------------
MIT + Commons Clause — Copyright (c) 2026 David Haz
https://github.com/DavidHDev/canvas-ui
The four Canvas Lab effects were re-implemented from documented behaviour.


--- mdb — "Up in the CloudSea" --------------------------------------------
Passed on through Rice-dog/code-codex (MIT) and
incarnation-gem/sunset-cloud-train.
The scene shader behind Cloud Train, kept as the starting point for SOFT LAB's
own take on the scene (parameters, palettes and post-processing are already
this project's own work).


--- Poly Haven — "PAV Studio 03" -----------------------------------------
CC0. Photographed by Grzegorz Wronkowski.
https://polyhaven.com/a/pav_studio_03
The environment map behind the Soft Matter experiment's refraction.
